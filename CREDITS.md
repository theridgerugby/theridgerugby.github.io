## Design and interaction

- The Liquid Glass material, theatre frame, scroll timeline, dialogs, and scene compositions are authored for this project. The glass system is implemented through `GlassSurface`, `GlassButton`, `GlassLink`, and shared CSS material layers.
- `BlurText` follows the word-reveal interaction pattern documented in the project prompts and popularized by [React Bits](https://reactbits.dev) (MIT); no React Bits source code was copied.
- [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) provides interface icons through `lucide-react` (ISC).
- GSAP ScrollTrigger supplies pinning and scrub coordination under the GSAP standard license; no paid plugins are used.

## Third-party libraries

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, GSAP, Lucide, and Oxlint are listed with exact resolved versions in `package-lock.json` and retain their respective open-source licenses.

## Media

The film, stag, research figures, and family photographs are the user's own or commissioned source assets. Gallery stills and the favicon are crops extracted from those authored assets; no third-party stock imagery was added.
