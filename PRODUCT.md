# PRODUCT.md — Ethan Gan · personal site

**Register:** brand. The design is the product: a cinematic, scroll-driven autobiographical play.

## What it is

A single-page story guided by a luminous stag. On desktop, scrolling advances a film in eight acts:

1. Overture — family photographs become early memory.
2. The question — a Chinese WeChat exchange about leaving for Canada.
3. Arrival — Toronto Pearson becomes a threshold.
4. Life here — a real family-archive frame is projected like a home movie.
5. Blue memoirs — moving image freezes into a desk of family photographs.
6. Photography — visual studies from the film become a three-dimensional gallery.
7. Research — two attempts to understand beauty, disagreement, and ambiguity.
8. Curtain — Ethan is named; visitors can email him, continue to GitHub, or replay the film.

This is not a portfolio grid. It is a film you scroll.

## Audience and mood

The primary cinematic viewer is on a laptop, at night, leaning in. The mood is intimate, theatrical, nostalgic, and quietly mysterious.

The story must remain complete for people on phones, tablets, zoomed browser layouts, or reduced-motion settings. Those visitors receive a linear, image-led reading experience rather than a rejection screen.

## Non-negotiables

- **Content is the stage; interface is glass.** Videos, photographs, research figures, and the stag remain visually direct. Only controls and viewing surfaces use Liquid Glass.
- The stag is one persistent independent overlay in cinematic mode. It is baked into the opening world, hands off to the stage companion, and expands into the final hero.
- The stage remains near-black. Scene lighting carries the color: lavender and gold, cool white, amber, cobalt, and moonlit blue.
- Scroll drives the desktop performance. Motion is restrained, ease-out-expo, and never bouncy.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is a first-class mode, not a partial animation override.
- Autobiographical media and metadata must be truthful. No placeholder works, invented locations, or stock stand-ins.
- The Chinese WeChat scene stays visually Chinese. An English translation is available semantically in the film and visibly in reading mode.
- Core interactions must be native and operable by keyboard: buttons, links, focus-managed dialogs, Escape, and visible focus.
- No persistent navigation. The story ends with only three actions: GitHub, Email, and Replay.

## Typography

- Display and intertitles: Spectral Light italic, self-hosted for a quiet, diaphanous film-title voice.
- Narrative body: Hanken Grotesk 350/400/500, self-hosted for an open, human rhythm.
- Technical metadata: JetBrains Mono.
- Chinese scene: Noto Sans SC.

## Current boundaries

- No audio.
- No CV link until a real, publishable CV is supplied.
- The high-resolution Pearson and stag footage is intentionally preserved; mobile and reduced-motion modes avoid loading it.
- Family photographs are part of the authored narrative. They require an explicit privacy check before any public deployment.
